About Us

Rooted in Kibiito, Growing Together

A school that shaped generations — now a community that keeps the spirit alive.

Our School, Our Family

Moset Nursery and Primary School has been a home of learning in Kibiito, Bunyangabu. We celebrate every learner — past and present.

  • Nursery and full primary (P1–P7) education
  • Strong OBs & OGs network across Uganda
  • Community-driven support and mentorship
  • Digital platform by Moses Software LTD

A Place to Belong

Whether you were here for nursery, primary, or both — you are part of the Moset story. This platform exists to reconnect OBs and OGs, share updates, and give back to the school that shaped us.

Founded on values of discipline, excellence, and community, Moset continues to nurture young minds in Kibiito, Bunyangabu District. Our online home makes it easier to stay in touch, no matter where life takes you.
